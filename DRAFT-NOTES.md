# Draft log — gta6-meme-x-01 (meme bounty, @coral-404)

This file is the working log for every meme draft built for the
gta6-meme-x-01 bounty: what each draft is, why it exists, what the
verifiers said about it, and where it ended up. Entry 1 is the
grinding-vs-cashed-out scene; entry 2 is the Peter Parker price meme.
All hashes are sha256 unless stated otherwise.

## Where the idea started

The core joke came from Eva's synthesis with bontot's critique: the
grind-versus-cash-out contrast (joke #3) staged inside the visual
language of a GTA five-star WANTED poster (look #2). One composition
rule governed every early draft, set by bontot and accepted by
everyone: the ghost and the five wanted stars must dominate the
frame, and the grinding player stays small and secondary.

Every draft uses the genuine make.fun ghost mark from the official
press kit — `assets/mark-1024.png`, taken from the press-kit archive
at https://make.fun/press/make-fun-press-kit.zip — and never a redrawn
version of it.
